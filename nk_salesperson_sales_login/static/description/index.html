Field Salesperson Portal

Give your field sales team private quotations, expense tracking, surveys and live location tracking, all inside Odoo.

Why use this module?

  • Every salesperson sees only their own quotations and orders.
  • Managers know where the team is and what they sold.
  • Expenses, visits and surveys are recorded on the field, not on paper.
  • Profit and category reports are ready in one click.

Key Features

Sales

  • Salesperson login – private quotations and orders per salesperson.
  • Quotation rejection wizard – reject a quotation with a reason.
  • Sales, category and profit reports – with PDF statements.

Expenses

  • Expense management – salespersons record and track field expenses.

Field Work

  • Surveys – collect customer feedback and visit information.
  • Live location tracking – track salesperson visits and locations.

How it works

  1. The manager creates a salesperson login and links it to an employee.
  2. The salesperson creates quotations, logs expenses and fills surveys.
  3. Location is tracked during visits.
  4. The manager reviews reports, expenses and team activity.
