Field Salesperson Portal

Private quotations, reporting, expenses, surveys and live location tracking for your field sales team

Key Features

  • Salesperson login – each salesperson sees only their own quotations and orders.
  • Sales reports – sales, category and profit reports with PDF statements.
  • Expense management – salespersons record and track field expenses.
  • Surveys – collect customer feedback and visit information on the field.
  • Live location tracking – track salesperson visits and locations.
  • Quotation rejection wizard – reject quotations with a reason.

Requirements

Depends on: Sales (sale_management), Sales Margin (sale_margin) and Employees (hr).
